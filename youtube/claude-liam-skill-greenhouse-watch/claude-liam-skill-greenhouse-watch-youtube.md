greenhouse-watch — Your Board, Your Call

greenhouse-watch fetches one Greenhouse job board, diffs it against a state file, matches new postings to a JSON résumé on a documented rule-set, and stops. This teardown shows the six-step pipeline, the NoRedirect fetcher, the baseline→diff state cycle, the scheme weights you can apply by hand, the real Airbnb output (163 jobs, 25 new, 7 relevant), and the gate line that keeps the deciding with the human.

Every factual claim in this video was checked against primary sources before rendering.
