# Every open job at Canva — 2026-09-19

## Executive summary

**What this is.** All 248 jobs Canva had open on its public careers board on September 19, 2026, one card each, grouped by department. Every card has the same eight fields (company, title, link, location, department, dates, posted pay band, job id) so they can be compared side by side. No filtering, no scoring — this is the whole board.

**Why read it.** The matched list (`…-TENTATIVE.md`) shows only the postings that mention words on my CV, and I do not trust a word-match to decide what I never see. This is the complete set, so I can scan the titles myself and pull anything the rules missed into the keep list (`…-KEEP.md`).

**What is here.** 38 departments. Information Technology → Platform is the largest (68 postings), then Sales → Go To Market (30). 47 of 248 are fully remote; 169 are hybrid at a hub. 7 post a pay band. 44 postings are contract, contingent, or otherwise not full-time employment. Canva's board is Sydney-centred: 126 of 248 postings are in Australia, 35 in the US (sales, finance, legal, a few engineering). All 47 remote-flagged postings are outside the US. 23 are Contract and 43 are contingent workers — Canva hires on contract routinely, but every Education/Content role is in APAC. No Advocate, Developer Educator, or US education-content role exists on the board today.

**What this is not.** Not a shortlist and not a recommendation. Cards are copied from the company's own posting data; the only derived field is the remote/hub note.
