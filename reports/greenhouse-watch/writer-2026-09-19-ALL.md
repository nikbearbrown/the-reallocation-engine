# Every open job at Writer — 2026-09-19

## Executive summary

**What this is.** All 51 jobs Writer had open on its public careers board on September 19, 2026, one card each, grouped by department. Every card has the same eight fields (company, title, link, location, department, dates, posted pay band, job id) so they can be compared side by side. No filtering, no scoring — this is the whole board.

**Why read it.** The matched list (`…-TENTATIVE.md`) shows only the postings that mention words on my CV, and I do not trust a word-match to decide what I never see. This is the complete set, so I can scan the titles myself and pull anything the rules missed into the keep list (`…-KEEP.md`).

**What is here.** 15 departments. Delivery & customer success → Customer success is the largest (13 postings), then Engineering, product & design → Engineering (11). 0 of 51 are fully remote; 51 are hybrid at a hub. 33 post a pay band. 1 posting is part-time, contract, or temporary rather than full-time employment. Every Writer posting is hybrid at one of six hubs (SF, NYC, Seattle, Austin, Chicago, London); the board's remote flag is set on all 51 but no posting is remote-only. One scheme lesson: the words *generative AI*, *AI agents*, and *learning* appear in all 51 postings — Writer's boilerplate — and on a first pass they alone pushed every job over the line, including Senior Commercial Counsel. They are now ignored for this board; that is a fix to the rules, not to the CV.

**What this is not.** Not a shortlist and not a recommendation. Cards are copied from the company's own posting data; the only derived field is the remote/hub note.
