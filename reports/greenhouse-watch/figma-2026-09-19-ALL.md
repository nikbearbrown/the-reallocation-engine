# Every open job at Figma — 2026-09-19

## Executive summary

**What this is.** All 152 jobs Figma had open on its public careers board on September 19, 2026, one card each, grouped by department. Every card has the same eight fields (company, title, link, location, department, dates, posted pay band, job id) so they can be compared side by side. No filtering, no scoring — this is the whole board.

**Why read it.** The matched list (`…-TENTATIVE.md`) shows only the 55 postings that mention words on my CV, and I do not trust a word-match to decide what I never see. This is the complete set, so I can scan the titles myself and pull anything the rules missed into the keep list (`…-KEEP.md`).

**What is here.** 13 departments. Sales is the largest (56 postings), then Engineering (27). 80 of 152 can be held remotely in the United States. 93 post a pay band; the rest (mostly non-US) do not. There are no part-time, contract, or consulting postings.

**What this is not.** Not a shortlist and not a recommendation. Cards are copied from Figma's own posting data; the only derived field is the remote/hub note, taken from the posting's own sentence about where the role can be held.
